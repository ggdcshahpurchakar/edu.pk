Program Overview

About the ADS Program

The Associate Degree in Science (ADS) is a degree-level pathway intended for students who have completed intermediate education and meet the current admission requirements. The available subjects and study structure are determined by the affiliated university and current college offering.

Important: Please confirm whether ADS admissions are currently offered, the duration, subject combinations, affiliation, and admission process with the college before publishing these details as final.
