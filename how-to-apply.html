Degree Programs

ADS / BSc & ADA / BA

Apply Online

How to Apply

1
Online Application

Apply online through the SBBU E-Portal.

2
Document Submission

Submit the required academic documents and photographs as specified by the college.

Required Documents

  • ✓ Intermediate Pass Certificate (Photocopy)
  • ✓ Intermediate Marksheet (3 Photocopies)
  • ✓ Matriculation Pass Certificate (Photocopy)
  • ✓ Matriculation Marksheet (2 Photocopies)
  • ✓ B-Form (2 Photocopies)
  • ✓ 4 Passport-Size Photographs

Important: Applicants should carefully check the admission requirements and submit complete documents according to the instructions provided by the relevant admission portal and the college.
